Our Manufacturing & Turnkey Process
Execution in 4 Engineering Stages

From initial site feasibility and 3D structural CAD modeling to heavy-duty fabrication, on-site erection, and statutory safety handover.

1

Feasibility & 3D Design

Our structural team surveys your plot dimensions, column spans, and height clearances to generate optimal 2D/3D layout simulations.

2

Precision Fabrication

3

On-Site Erection

Certified installation engineers assemble steel columns, hydraulic power packs, traction motors, and micro-PLC control panels on-site.

4

Safety Audit & AMC
